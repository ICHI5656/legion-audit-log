## Update: Cache Root Cause Confirmed

  the plain branch raw URL.

  complete current content, including the top index and newest-first updates.

- `FIX-20260729-28` is closed.

- The top index and newest-first ordering remain as readability and
  defense-in-depth measures.

  implementation was performed.
- Status: closed.
